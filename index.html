Company

Shareholder, service provider, investor.

Vileplam Enterprises Ltd, based in Nicosia, Cyprus, owns its subsidiaries outright, provides services and financing to group companies, and invests in new technologies.

What we do

Shareholder of subsidiaries
Vileplam holds 100% of the shares in its subsidiaries. Each subsidiary is managed by its own board in its own jurisdiction.
Business services
Vileplam provides administrative and consulting services to group companies under service agreements, for a fee.
Group financing
Vileplam provides loans to group companies. It invests its own surplus funds in fiat, digital assets and funds.
Investing in new technologies
Vileplam invests in projects and companies in new technologies.

How we earn

  • Service & consulting feescharged to group companies for services provided.
  • Interest incomeon loans to group companies.
  • Dividendspaid by our subsidiaries.
  • Trading profitsfrom investing our own funds in digital assets.

Own funds only. Vileplam invests only its own capital. We do not accept or manage third-party money.
